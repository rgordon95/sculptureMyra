The work

Myra follows her ideas toward minimal abstraction, paying close attention to line and to negative space. The pieces are visualizations of mental processes and an expression of the character of each material, whether veined alabaster, Alaska soapstone or a length of black walnut.

She studied at the Barsanti Studio in Pietrasanta, Italy, and later in Carrara. Her sculptures are in private collections from New York to California and the Netherlands.
